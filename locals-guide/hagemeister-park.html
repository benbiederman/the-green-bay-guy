Hagemeister Park

Discover Hagemeister Park, a charming restaurant located in downtown Green Bay along the Fox River, right next to the CityDeck.

Hagemeister Park offers a diverse menu with options that appeal to a wide range of tastes. The restaurant also features two bars, one indoors and one outdoors, both offering excellent views of the river and CityDeck area. The outdoor bar especially makes for a great place to relax on a warm summer evening and enjoy the atmosphere.

Where Hagemeister Park stands out most is its location and overall environment. The food, however, has been a bit inconsistent in my experience. On one visit, we nearly ordered a menu item before learning it contained onions after checking with our waiter. We then switched to different dishes after being told they normally did not come with onions, but when the meals arrived, onions had still been added by the kitchen. The mistake was acknowledged, though the resolution was limited to a small discount on the bill.

That said, I have had better experiences there on other visits, and the atmosphere alone still makes it a worthwhile stop, especially during the summer months. Personally, if I’m looking for a stronger overall meal experience in the area, I’d lean toward Fox Harbor or Copper State Brewing, both located nearby down by Hagemeister. Then afterward, heading over to Hagemeister Park for drinks by the water is a great way to end the evening.
