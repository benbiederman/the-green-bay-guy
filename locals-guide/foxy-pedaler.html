Foxy Pedaler / Foxy Paddler

If you’re coming to Green Bay and looking for a unique way to experience the city, Foxy Pedaler and Foxy Paddler may be just what you’re looking for.

Recently, I had the opportunity to experience both, spending part of the evening riding through downtown Green Bay on the Foxy Pedaler before heading out on the Fox River with Foxy Paddler.

The Foxy Pedaler is a pedal-powered party bike that takes you through downtown Green Bay, but don't worry, it also has a motor. Along the way, you have opportunities to stop at local bars and restaurants. I've driven and walked through downtown countless times, but cruising through it on the Foxy Pedaler made for a completely different experience.

The Foxy Paddler is a pontoon boat, and while it’s not powered by pedals, they do have them on the pontoon, so you can keep burning those calories if you so choose.

I really enjoyed my experience with both the Foxy Pedaler and Foxy Paddler. With the Pedaler only going roughly 5 MPH, it was nice to slow down and actually enjoy the city around us as we cruised through downtown.

With the Foxy Paddler, it was my first time being on the Fox River in downtown Green Bay. Cruising along while looking at the cargo ships around us and the CityDeck with all the businesses along it was a relaxing experience, and one that I didn’t even realize how much I needed.

The Foxy Pedaler and Foxy Paddler are great activities for birthdays, bachelor or bachelorette parties, work outings, and other group events, but they’re also a great way to experience downtown Green Bay from a different perspective. I've spent plenty of time around downtown, but experiencing it this way gave me a new appreciation for the area.

If it’s something you’d like to experience but don’t have a group of friends readily available, you can also book as an individual rider and join others for the ride.

While we happened to have a beautiful summer night, Foxy Pedaler isn't limited to summer. Rides continue into the fall, with covers available to provide some protection when the weather isn't quite as cooperative.

Whether you're visiting Green Bay or you're a local looking for something different to do, Foxy Pedaler and Foxy Paddler are worth checking out. I hope to do it again in the future!
