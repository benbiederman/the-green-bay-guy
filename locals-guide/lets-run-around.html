Let's Run Around

If you’re a parent of young children, you know how hard it can be to burn off their energy during the dog days of summer, brutal winter months, or any of the other less-than-ideal weather situations we get here in Wisconsin. Thankfully, Let's Run Around opened in fall 2025 and brought a much-needed solution to the area.

The large indoor play space offers something for kids of all ages. There’s a designated area for children 2 and under, a block area where even parents can join in and build alongside their kids, swings, and multiple playground sections spread throughout the facility.

As a parent of a young toddler, I was excited when Let’s Run Around announced its opening. At the same time, I was a little skeptical about how they’d keep everything clean with so many kids coming through each day. To their credit, cleanliness is clearly a major priority, and they do an excellent job maintaining both the space and equipment.

When you arrive, there aren’t employees greeting you at the door. Instead, you purchase passes online and use a code to enter the building. Because of that, I’d recommend buying passes ahead of time so you’re not stuck waiting at the entrance.

Let’s Run Around also isn’t just for the kids. Inside the facility is Kompound Coffee, a great local coffee shop that helps parents stay energized while chasing their kids around.

One downside is that it can get extremely busy during bad weather months. If you have a younger child, the environment can feel a bit overwhelming with so many kids running around. You’re also relying on parents to actively supervise their children, which doesn’t always happen. Fortunately, the layout helps with that somewhat, since quieter areas like the 2-and-under section and block area offer a chance to step away from the chaos.

Overall, Let’s Run Around is a welcome addition to the Green Bay area. It’s become a regular stop for our family, and it’s a place I’m genuinely thankful to have available for our kids.
